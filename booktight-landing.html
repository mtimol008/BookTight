  2. 02

FAQ

Questions tradespeople ask

Who's it for?

Solo tradespeople in the UK, like electricians, plumbers and heating engineers, who run their own diary and drive to every job. If you're the one deciding when to say "I can do Tuesday", it's for you.

How does it pick a day?

It looks at the jobs you've already booked and works out how much extra distance the new job would add to each day, based on how far apart the addresses are. Then it suggests the day and time that adds the least, within your working hours and rules.

It also shows why the other days lost out, and you can always pick a different slot yourself.

Does it work with my calendar?

Yes. Turn on the calendar feed in Account and subscribe to it from Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook. Your Booktight jobs then show up there and stay up to date by themselves.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel any time from Account, with no minimum term. You keep full access until the end of the period you've paid for. After that:

  • Your account becomes read-only, and you can still export all your jobs.
  • Your calendar link keeps working for 30 days.
  • We keep your data for 90 days in case you come back, and warn you before we delete it.
Do you show ads or sell my data?

No. There are no ads, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising. See the privacy policy for the details.

Does it work outside the UK?

Not yet. Addresses, distances and pricing are all set up for the UK only for now.
